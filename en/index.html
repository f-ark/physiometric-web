How it works

📸

Measure

Take a front and a side photo. Body landmarks are detected and your alignment is calculated right on your phone.

📊

See your score

Get a clear Posture Score with easy-to-understand findings such as forward head posture or uneven shoulders.

🎯

Mark tension

Tap where you feel tight, and your routine adapts to your body map.

💪

Move

Follow a short daily routine with 3D animated guidance and voice coaching.

📈

Progress

Re-scan every 3 days, compare your score and keep your streak going.

Who is it for?

Desk workers, students and anyone who wants to stand taller, move better and build a daily mobility habit. No equipment or prior experience needed.

🔒 Privacy first

Posture analysis runs on your phone. Your photos and results never leave your device and are not sent to our servers.
