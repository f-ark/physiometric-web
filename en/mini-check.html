Mini check: head and shoulder alignment

Try two of the app's measurements here. All you need to do is face the camera.

🔒 The image never leaves your device. The measurement runs in your browser; nothing is sent or saved.

How to stand

  • Face the camera with your arms relaxed at your sides.
  • For your head, your face just needs to be visible.
  • For your shoulders, step back a few paces so your ankles are visible too.
  • Keep the light in front of you, not a window behind you.

This is a single quick look. The app looks at your whole posture from two photos, front and side, and builds a movement plan around you.

The full check and your plan are in the app
