Delete Account

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Important: Deleting your account cannot be undone. Server records linked to your account are permanently deleted.

Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the PosMetric app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

  1. Write from the email address you signed in with.
  2. Address: info@physiometric.app
  3. Subject: "Account Deletion Request - PosMetric"

Once your request is verified, your data is deleted within 48 hours.

What happens when you delete your account?

DataWhat happens
Sign-in dataFirebase AuthenticationPermanently deleted as soon as the request is processed.
Free allowance counterRecord linked to your IDDeleted. An anonymous device hash that does not identify you may be kept to enforce the free allowance.
Measurement and workout dataPhotos, results, recordsNever stored on our servers. It stays on your phone and is fully deleted when you uninstall the app.
Crash logsFirebase CrashlyticsTechnical crash records are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Subscription: Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (or the App Store on iOS).
